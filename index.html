About Akilan Constructions

We are a building construction and consulting company based in Manadikuppam, Panruti. Since 2023 we have designed, got approved and built homes, shops, schools and hospitals for families and institutions across Cuddalore district.

Our approach is simple: one team that handles drawings, approvals, loans and construction, so you never chase four different people for one building.

DCE., BE | Licensed Civil Engineer | DRDA Government Contractor

Er. R. Arul leads every project with 9 years of field experience in building construction and consulting. He registered as an Engineer, Grade III (RE) with Panruti Municipality, and started Akilan Constructions on 24 July 2023.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle building plan approval?

Yes. We prepare the plan and elevation with approval blueprint drawings and apply online or offline, depending on what your local body needs.

Can you help me get a housing loan?

Yes. We act as housing loan consultants and help you prepare the drawings, estimates and documents banks ask for.

Do you build only houses?

No. We build residential and commercial buildings as well as schools and hospitals.

Will the house follow Vastu?

If you want it to. We design Vastu-friendly modern house plans and adjust them to your plot.

Do you do renovation work?

Yes. We renovate and extend existing buildings, and also do elevation, interior and exterior work.

Documents to keep ready for plan approval

These are the papers usually asked for. We confirm the exact list for your plot on the first visit.

Ownership document

Sale deed or settlement deed of the plot.

Patta / chitta

Latest copy from the revenue records.

Encumbrance certificate

EC showing the plot is free of claims.

Property tax receipt

Most recent tax receipt for the plot.

Owner ID and address proof

Aadhaar or any government-issued ID.

Site details

Photos and rough measurements of the land.
