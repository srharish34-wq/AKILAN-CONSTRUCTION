About Akilan Constructions

Engineer-led building construction and consulting in Panruti, built on good ideas and honest work.

About our company

Akilan Constructions is a building construction and consulting company based in Manadikuppam, Panruti. We started on 24 July 2023 with one aim: give families and institutions a single, reliable team for everything a building needs.

We prepare plans and elevations, get them approved, help with housing loans, and then build, renovate or value your property.

  • Residential, commercial, school and hospital buildings
  • Building plan, elevation and approval blueprints
  • Valuation, renovation and Vastu modern house plans
  • Housing loan consultancy

Our founder, Er. R. Arul

Founder and Licensed Civil Engineer

Er. R. Arul has 9 years of experience in building construction and consulting. He is a DRDA government contractor and is registered with Panruti Municipality as an Engineer, Grade III (RE).

He stays close to every site, so the drawings you approve are the building you get.
