    How we work with you

    1. 1

      Site visit and brief

      We visit your plot, listen to your needs and budget, and check the land.

    2. 2

      Design and drawings

      Plan, elevation, structural and Vastu drawings, shared with you for changes.

    3. 3

      Approval and loan

      We file for building plan approval and help prepare your loan documents.

    4. 4

      Construction

      Work starts under engineer supervision with regular progress updates.

    5. 5

      Final checks, finishing touches and a clean handover of your building.

    Service questions

    Can I take just one service?

    Yes. You can ask for a single service, such as a plan, a valuation or loan guidance, or hand us the whole project. Tell us what you need.

    Do you handle plan approval online and offline?

    Yes. We prepare the drawings and apply through the online or offline route, whichever your local body requires.

    Which types of buildings do you build?

    Residential and commercial buildings, schools and hospitals, plus renovation of existing buildings.

    Can you help with a loan for construction?

    Yes. We guide you through housing and building construction loans and help prepare the drawings, estimates and documents.

    How do I get a quote?

    Call 96298 56936 or send an enquiry. We will visit the site, discuss your needs and share a plan and quote. You can also try the cost estimator on our home page.

    Not sure which service you need?

    Tell us about your plot or building. We will suggest the right starting point.
